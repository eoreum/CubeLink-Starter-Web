# Third-party software

`dist/vendor/blockly.min.js` and `dist/vendor/ko.js` are the Blockly files vendored in [CubeLink Studio](https://github.com/tjind/Cubelink-studio/tree/main/libs/blockly). Blockly is a Google project licensed under Apache License 2.0; upstream copyright and license notices are retained in the source files. See https://github.com/google/blockly and https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 . CubeLink Starter defines its own limited Arduino lesson blocks and reuses the Studio's standard renderer, C-shaped setup/loop layout, and category colors.
